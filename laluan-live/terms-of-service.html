1. What LaluanLive Is

LaluanLive is a community-driven tool that shows live status information for Klang Valley rail lines. It combines official rail operator notices, weather forecasts and warnings published by MET Malaysia, and reports submitted by riders. LaluanLive is an independent project. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any rail operator, MET Malaysia, JPS or any government agency. Line, station and agency names are used for identification only.

2. Not an Official Source

Information shown in the App, including line statuses, incident reports, estimated repair or resume times, weather warnings, forecasts and rain indicators, is provided for general awareness only and may be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. In particular:

  • Official notices are relayed from the rail operator's public announcements and may lag behind, or leave out details of, the original.
  • Rider reports, including "Raining here" reports and the rain indicators built from them, are unverified.
  • Suggestions for other ways to travel come from the line map and live status, not from the rail operator.
  • The map is an illustration of the network and the situation. It is not to scale, positions are approximate, and it must not be used for navigation.

Always refer to official rail operator channels, station announcements and staff for time-critical decisions, such as whether to catch a specific train or how to plan around a disruption. We are not responsible for any loss, delay, missed connection or damage arising from reliance on information shown in the App.

3. Weather and Flood Information

LaluanLive is not an official warning service. Weather information is based on forecasts and warnings published by MET Malaysia through data.gov.my, matched to stations by district. It may be delayed, incomplete or matched to stations inaccurately. A warning shown for a station is an area warning: it does not mean that the station, its exits or its surroundings are flooded, and it does not show flood depth. LaluanLive does not give emergency instructions. Follow MetMalaysia, JPS/InfoBanjir and NADMA, and in an emergency call 999.

6. How Reports Close

Rider reports are not moderated or flagged by other users. A report closes automatically once the line has seen no new activity for a set period, when enough different riders confirm the line is running fine, or when the rail operator announces that service is back. Rain indicators clear automatically a short time after the last "Raining here" report. Closed reports are deleted as described in Section 8.

7. Reporting Limits

To keep the App fair and reliable, the number of reports a device can submit is limited (for example, one report per line within a few minutes, and a maximum per hour). These limits apply to every device equally and are tied only to an anonymous device identifier. Separately, we may restrict or remove access for abuse of the App as described in Section 14.

8. Data Retention

Reports are retained only while relevant and for a limited period afterwards, and "Raining here" reports for a shorter period, as described in our Privacy Policy. We reserve the right to remove any content at any time, for any reason, including violations of these Terms.
